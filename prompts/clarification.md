# Clarification

All new sources of this update are read. Below are the issues that are still open. Each of them ends in exactly one of these:

- `write_mail`: the question goes to the supplier. One mail per supplier with all its questions, in the supplier's language. Ask only what the supplier can answer, and name order, part, quantity and date. Say by when the answer is needed. Keep the customer's internal matters (stock, other suppliers, people, approvals) out of supplier mails.
- `create_task`: someone at the customer has to check or decide something. Give it to the responsible role from the organisation chart and the approval rules.
- `dismiss`: nobody needs to do anything.

An issue raised by the action agent is a question it needs answered for a decision that is being prepared. Put it into the mail to the supplier it names, with the other questions for that supplier, and keep its wording precise. Do not dismiss it.

Use the lookup tools when an issue needs more context.
