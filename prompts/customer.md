# Customer: Soony

Soony Interactive GmbH is a games publisher in Munich. It makes nothing itself: suppliers deliver the parts, and every copy is packed at Soony's distribution center in Hamburg (site DC-HAM). There is no other site.

## The launch

- Grand Heist 6 goes on sale on Sunday 22 November 2026 at 20:00 (Hamburg time). The date is fixed by retail and by pre-orders.
- Trucks to retail leave Hamburg from Sunday 20:00. A copy that is not packed by then misses launch day.
- Two editions: the Standard Edition and the numbered Collector's Edition. Collector's copies are all pre-ordered.
- Packing shifts: Saturday 06:00 to 22:00 and Sunday 06:00 to 20:00. No night shift.
- Packed means packed at the distribution center in Hamburg. A part counts only once it is there and released for use.

## How Soony works

- Six suppliers, in Korea, China, India, the United States and Poland. They write in their own language (Korean, Chinese, Polish, English). Nobody in procurement reads Polish or Korean.
- SAP holds the purchase orders and nothing else. There are two exports because the interface changed in mid-2025.
- Open shipments are tracked in a shared spreadsheet that is updated when someone remembers.
- The receiving team in Hamburg scans delivery notes and books what is on the paper.
- Contracts, order confirmations, notices and delivery notes arrive as PDFs and scans in each supplier's folder of the portal.
- Who decides what is in Soony's organisation chart and its approval policy, which are among the documents.

## What the team wants

- To learn about a problem before the supplier tells them. "We find out at the dock."
- The true picture when sources disagree. Neither SAP nor the spreadsheets are reliably right.
- Decisions with a price on them, not more tables. One page per day on what can stop a line.
- Suppliers are contacted by e-mail. A question to a supplier goes out without waiting for a person. Anything that binds Soony (an order, an accepted offer, a freight booking, a quality release) needs the approval of the person the approval policy names.
